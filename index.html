The Love Chronicles

A Tale of Two Hearts

❧
By Destiny | First Published in Love

Zimri and Shawn first met on a friend's camping trip. He had almost skipped the trip, and she was not immediately convinced, but for him it was love at first sight. Somewhere between late-night fires, shared jokes, and quiet moments under the stars, he slowly won her over, and their story truly began.

Through the seasons, they have shared countless adventures - from spontaneous road trips to quiet Sunday mornings. They have grown together, supported each other's wildest dreams, and built a love that feels like coming home.

"In you, I've found my person, my partner, my forever. Every love story is beautiful, but ours is my favorite."
— Zimri & Shawn

Now, as they stand on the threshold of their greatest adventure, they invite their loved ones to witness the next chapter of their love story. Surrounded by the people who matter most, they will say "I do" and begin their happily ever after.
